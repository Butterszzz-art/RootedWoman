Resources · Nutrition Guide

Nutrition Basics

Simple habits that make a real difference. No calorie counting. No complicated plans. No perfection required.

You do not need a perfect diet. You need a sustainable one. Read through the whole guide first. Then pick two or three habits to start with. Add more as those become natural.

Section 1 — Your Quick Wins

Start here. These habits alone will change how you feel. Highest impact, lowest effort — no special food required.

💧
Quick Win 1 — Drink 2 litres of water every day

Most people are dehydrated and don't know it. Fatigue, headaches, hunger, poor focus, and cravings are often simply your body asking for water. Start your morning with a large glass before coffee or food. Finish 1.5 litres before 3pm — the rest comes easily.

🥤
Quick Win 2 — Eliminate sugary drinks and sodas

Sodas, fruit juices, energy drinks, and sweetened coffees are some of the biggest sources of hidden sugar. They spike your blood sugar, which leads to energy crashes and cravings. Replace with water, sparkling water, unsweetened herbal tea, or black coffee. Start by eliminating one per day.

🍔
Quick Win 3 — Reduce junk food and fast food

Fast food is engineered to make you want more — this is not a willpower problem. You don't need to eliminate it forever. If you currently eat fast food 5 times per week, reduce to 3. Then 2. Then 1. Small reductions done consistently beat dramatic cuts that don't last.

🍳
Quick Win 4 — Reduce fried food

Frying dramatically increases calorie content and introduces fats that contribute to inflammation and low energy. When you cook at home, choose grilling or baking. When eating out, choose grilled where possible. Make a better choice more often than not.

🛒
Quick Win 5 — Reduce processed and packaged food

A simple rule: if a product has more than 5 ingredients, or ingredients you can't pronounce, eat it less often. Replace packaged snacks with whole foods: fruit, nuts, boiled eggs, vegetables with hummus.

🥣
Quick Win 6 — Eat a real breakfast

Skipping breakfast sets your blood sugar on a rollercoaster for the rest of the day. A breakfast with protein keeps you fuller longer and gives you stable energy. Easy options: 2 eggs with vegetables, Greek yoghurt with fruit, oats with nuts, or a protein smoothie.

🥩
Quick Win 7 — Include protein in every meal

Protein is the building block of muscle and keeps you fuller for longer. Good sources: chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yoghurt, legumes, cottage cheese, tofu. You don't need to count grams — just make sure it's on the plate.

🥦
Quick Win 8 — Add vegetables to at least two meals per day

Vegetables keep your digestive system healthy, help you feel full, and stabilise blood sugar. You don't need to eat salad every day. Add spinach to eggs, put cucumber and tomato on the side of your lunch, roast a tray of vegetables for dinner. Small additions add up.

Section 3 — Reduce or Avoid

Not forever. Just less often than before. Progress is the goal, not perfection.

✕ Sugary drinks and sodas — Fanta, Pepsi, fruit juice, energy drinks, sweetened iced tea
✕ Fried food — anything deep-fried or pan-fried in large amounts of oil
✕ Junk food and fast food — burgers, hot dogs, chips, fried chicken, pizza
✕ Packaged and processed snacks — crisps, cookies, flavoured crackers, instant noodles
✕ Excess sugar — sweets, desserts, sugary cereals, flavoured yoghurts with added sugar
✕ Alcohol — it disrupts sleep, recovery, and hormonal balance. If you drink, drink less.

A useful question before eating: Does this food give me energy, or take it from me? Answer honestly and choose accordingly.

Section 4 — Practical Tips

Knowing what to eat is the easy part. These tips bridge the gap between knowing and doing.

🥡 Meal preparation

You don't need to meal prep for hours on Sunday. Even 30 minutes makes a difference. Boil eggs, wash and cut vegetables, cook a batch of rice. Having ready food removes the decision and the temptation.

🛒 Never shop hungry

Grocery shopping on an empty stomach leads to buying food you didn't plan to buy. Eat first, then shop. And write a list before you go.

⚖️ The 80/20 approach

Eat well 80% of the time. The other 20% is life — a birthday, a dinner out, a craving. This is not failure. This is balance. Make a good choice more often than a poor one.

🔍 Read labels

Start noticing the sugar content on labels. If sugar is listed in the first three ingredients, or if the product has more than 10g of sugar per 100g, choose a different option or eat it less often.

🍽️ Eat slowly

Your brain takes about 20 minutes to register that your stomach is full. Put down your fork between bites. Eat without your phone. Chew properly. These small things make a significant difference over time.

🕐 Don't skip meals

Skipping meals to compensate slows your metabolism, increases cravings, and almost always leads to overeating later. Eat regular meals. Keep portions sensible. Stay consistent.

You need to eat better than you did yesterday."
